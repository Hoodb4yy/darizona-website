About Darizona Studio

Darizona Studio is a photography and videography studio based in Entebbe. We started with a simple idea: the best pictures happen when people forget the camera is there.

Over the years we have covered weddings, birthdays, corporate events and commercial projects across Uganda. Every job gets the same care, whether it is a family celebration or a brand campaign.

We shoot, edit and deliver everything ourselves, so the people you meet at the shoot are the same people who finish your photos and films. You get natural, honest images and a timeline you can count on.
